Most families skim a biodata. A single, tidy page is more likely to be read fully, printed easily and forwarded without losing quality on a phone screen.

## Why one page wins

- Readers see everything at once
- It prints on a single sheet
- It looks confident, not cluttered
- Screenshots and forwards stay legible

## When two pages make sense

Two pages are fine if the extra content is useful, for example a large family with several siblings, a longer About me, or a detailed profession. In that case:

1. Put the photo, personal details, education, work and contact on the **first page**
2. Let family detail, astrology and expectations continue on the second
3. Avoid a nearly empty second page with one or two lines

## How to shorten a long biodata

- Combine lifestyle answers into fewer rows
- Cut duplicates, such as native place repeated in three sections
- Keep About me to three or four sentences
- Turn off sections you do not need. Hidden sections leave no gap.

## What happens when you download

BioCraft lays the biodata on a real A4 page. If it is longer, the preview shows a dashed line where page two begins. When you download a PDF, page breaks are placed between rows and paragraphs so nothing is cut in half, and a small margin is added to continuation pages.
