A biodata works best when every line earns its place. The list below separates what almost every family expects from what is optional.

## Essential details

- **Full name** and a **recent photo**
- **Date of birth** (age can be worked out from it)
- **Height** and **marital status**
- **Highest qualification**
- **Occupation** and place of work
- **Parents' names and occupations**
- **Contact person and phone number**

## Helpful details

- Time and place of birth, if your family matches horoscopes
- Siblings, with one short detail each
- Family type and native place
- Annual income (as a figure or range, if you are comfortable)
- Languages you speak
- Food preference

## Optional details

- Religion, community, gotra, rashi, nakshatra, manglik status
- Hobbies and interests
- Smoking and drinking habits, answered honestly
- A short About me
- Partner expectations

## How much is too much

If the page feels crowded, the first thing to cut is repetition. A family reads the biodata in under two minutes, so short values and clear headings matter more than volume.

## A quick way to check

Read your biodata as a stranger would. Could they tell who you are, what you do, where your family is from and how to reach you? If yes, it is complete.
