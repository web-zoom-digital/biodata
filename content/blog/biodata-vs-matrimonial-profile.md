The two are often mentioned together, but they do different jobs.

## What a biodata is

A biodata is a one- or two-page document. You decide what goes in it and who receives it. It is usually a PDF or image sent over messaging apps, email or printed for a meeting.

## What a matrimonial profile is

A matrimonial profile is a page on a website or app. It is visible to that platform's members, often searchable, and shaped by the platform's form fields and rules.

## Side by side

| | Biodata | Matrimonial profile |
|---|---|---|
| Who controls it | You | You and the platform |
| Who sees it | Only people you send it to | Members of the platform |
| Format | PDF, image or print | Web page |
| Fields | Anything you choose | Fixed form fields |
| Best for | Sharing with known families and relatives | Discovering new introductions |

## When a biodata is the better choice

- A relative suggests a family and you want to send something quickly
- You are meeting at a family gathering and want a printed page
- You prefer not to publish personal details on a public platform

## When a profile helps

- You want to reach people beyond your own network
- You want search filters for location, education or community

## Getting the best of both

Create your biodata first. It forces you to decide what to share and how to say it, and you can reuse that wording on any platform. Then share the biodata directly with the families you choose.
