The photo is the first thing a reader looks at. A good one makes the biodata feel personal and trustworthy.

## What makes a good biodata photo

- **Recent.** Taken within the last year or two, so you look as you do today.
- **Clear.** Sharp, in focus and not heavily compressed by a messaging app.
- **Well lit.** Natural daylight from a window or outdoors, with the light on your face.
- **Just you.** No one else in the frame.
- **Natural.** A relaxed expression rather than a stiff pose.

## Background and clothing

A plain or softly blurred background keeps attention on your face. Wear something simple that you would wear to a family event. Strong patterns and very bright colours can distract on a small photo.

## Framing

Head and shoulders, or head to waist, works best. Full-length photos make the face too small once the picture is placed in a biodata. Keep your eyes roughly a third of the way down from the top.

## Avoid

- Sunglasses or hats that hide your face
- Heavy filters or editing
- Selfies taken very close to the camera, which distort features
- Old photos
