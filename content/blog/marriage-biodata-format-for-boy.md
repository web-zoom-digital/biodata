When two families begin conversations for an arranged marriage, the marriage biodata is almost always the first document shared. For a groom, a well-crafted biodata acts as an authentic reflection of his personal background, professional achievements, and family heritage.

The primary goal of a boy's marriage biodata is clarity and transparency. It should highlight who you are, what you do, and your family background without overwhelming the reader with unnecessary fluff or cluttered design.

## Recommended section order for a boy's biodata

Most Indian families read a matrimony biodata in a logical top-to-bottom sequence. Adhering to this established layout helps the reader find key information instantly:

| Section Name | What to Include | Best Practices & Tips |
|---|---|---|
| **Header & Photo** | Full Name, Religious Symbol/Heading, Recent Photo | Use a clear HD portrait with a soft smile and neutral background. |
| **Personal Details** | Date of Birth, Time of Birth, Place of Birth, Height, Complexion, Marital Status | Write height in feet and inches (e.g., 5' 10"). |
| **Education** | Highest Degree, Specialization, College/University | State highest qualification first; keep school details optional. |
| **Profession & Career** | Designation, Organization Name, Work City, Annual Income | Clearly state your current role and city of employment. |
| **Family Background** | Father's Name & Occupation, Mother's Name & Occupation, Siblings, Native Place | Specify marital status and occupation of siblings clearly. |
| **Horoscope & Religion** | Religion, Caste/Sub-caste, Gotra, Rashi, Nakshatra, Manglik Status | Include only if horoscope matching is important to your family. |
| **Contact Details** | Primary Contact Person, Phone Number, Email Address, Residential City | Give the phone number of a parent or guardian who responds promptly. |
| **About Me & Expectations** | 2–3 sentences on lifestyle, hobbies, and partner expectations | Keep it positive, genuine, and focused on core personal values. |

## Detailed breakdown of key sections

### 1. Personal statistics and physical details
Provide your exact date of birth, place of birth, and time of birth. If horoscope matching is observed in your community, exact birth time is essential for Kundali preparation. Include height in standard format (e.g., 5 ft 10 in / 178 cm). Complexion (e.g., Fair, Wheatish) and marital status (Never Married) are standard fields.

### 2. Education and professional background
Highlight your highest qualification prominently—such as B.Tech in Computer Science, MBA in Finance, or MD in Internal Medicine—along with the institution name.

For your profession, specify your designation (e.g., Senior Software Engineer at Tech Firm, Assistant Vice President at Public Bank, or Civil Engineer in State Govt) along with your current work location. If comfortable, include your annual CTC or income range. Clear career trajectory builds confidence for prospective bride's parents.

### 3. Family background & values
Family details hold significant weight in traditional proposals. State your father's name and occupation (e.g., Retired Govt Officer, Businessman, Senior Advocate) and mother's details (e.g., Homemaker, High School Teacher). Mention your siblings with their marital status and occupations (e.g., 1 Elder Sister - Married, settled in Bengaluru; 1 Younger Brother - Pursuing M.Tech). Mentioning your native town or ancestral place offers valuable context for cultural alignment.

### 4. Horoscope and astrological details (optional)
If your family practices astrology matching, list Rashi (Moon sign), Nakshatra, Gotra, and Manglik status (e.g., Non-Manglik / Anshik Manglik). If your family does not follow astrology, feel free to omit this section entirely.

### 5. Writing a warm 'About Me' and partner preferences
Avoid generic resume phrases like "hardworking and goal-oriented." Instead, share genuine glimpses of your personality:
> *"I am an easygoing tech professional settled in Pune with a passion for weekend trekking, classical music, and cooking. I value family relationships, mutual respect, and open communication."*

For partner expectations, express your values clearly:
> *"Looking for an educated, understanding partner with a warm personality and family-oriented mindset, who values both personal aspirations and joint decision-making."*

## Common formatting mistakes to avoid

- **Using outdated or low-quality photos**: Avoid selfies, cropped group photos, or low-resolution images.
- **Overcrowding details**: Adding multi-paragraph descriptions makes the document difficult to read. Stick to structured bullet points.
- **Missing or incorrect phone numbers**: Always double-check contact numbers and provide the name of the primary contact person (e.g., Father / Uncle / Self).
- **Misrepresenting facts**: Be accurate regarding education, job titles, and family background to ensure long-term trust.
