About me is the only place in a biodata where your voice comes through. A few clear sentences can make the whole page feel warmer.

## A simple formula

1. **Who you are** in one line: your nature or how friends describe you
2. **What you value**: family, honesty, learning, service
3. **What you enjoy**: one or two specific interests
4. **What you hope for**: the kind of home life you want

## Example paragraphs

**Professional, family-minded**

"I am a calm and dependable person who enjoys my work as an engineer and my weekends with family. I value honesty and good conversation. I like cooking on Sundays and short treks, and I hope to build a warm, respectful home."

**Creative, independent**

"I teach art to school children and paint in my spare time. I am curious, patient and close to my family. I hope to share a life with someone kind, who enjoys learning as much as I do."

**Simple and direct**

"I am easygoing and family-oriented. I enjoy music, travel and spending time with friends and relatives. I believe in respect and open communication."

## Words and habits to avoid

- Stacks of adjectives such as "smart, beautiful, caring, ambitious"
- Copying a paragraph from someone else's biodata
- Comparisons or claims about others
- Very long paragraphs

## Make it specific

"I like to travel" is common. "I have visited all the hill stations in Himachal and plan to see Kerala next" is memorable. One concrete detail is worth more than five general ones.
