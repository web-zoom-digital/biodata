Making a high-quality marriage biodata does not require advanced design skills, expensive software, or account registrations. With our privacy-focused online biodata builder, you can create a structured, professional document in minutes.

Follow this step-by-step master guide to build, preview, and download your marriage biodata.

## Phase 1: Preparation & Information Gathering

Before opening the editor, gather the following details to ensure smooth typing:

1. **Personal Information**: Full legal name, date of birth, exact time of birth, birth city/state, height, and marital status.
2. **Educational Qualifications**: Highest degree obtained, specialization, college or university name, and graduation year (optional).
3. **Professional Background**: Current job title, employer or company name, work location, and annual income or salary range.
4. **Family Background**: Father's name & occupation, mother's name & occupation, details of brothers and sisters (including marital status and occupations), native town, and family type (nuclear or joint).
5. **Horoscope Details (Optional)**: Religion, caste/sub-caste, gotra, rashi, nakshatra, and manglik status (if customary in your community).
6. **Contact Information**: Primary contact person's name (e.g. Father / Self), active mobile phone number, email address, and residential city.
7. **Profile Photo**: A high-resolution front-facing portrait photograph with a pleasant smile.

## Phase 2: Using the Online Biodata Editor

### Step 1: Open the editor and enter personal statistics
Navigate to the free [biodata editor](/create). Enter your full name and date of birth. Our editor automatically calculates your exact age from your birth date, so your document never displays outdated age numbers.

### Step 2: Fill in education, career, and family details
Proceed through the editor sections systematically. Use the **Add Brother** and **Add Sister** buttons to list siblings individually. If there are sections you prefer not to display, toggle them off or leave fields blank—empty fields are automatically omitted from the output.

### Step 3: Upload and crop your photograph
Click the photo upload button and select a JPG, PNG, or WebP image from your device. Use the interactive cropping wheel to position and scale your face nicely within the standard 4:5 frame. For photography recommendations, read our guide on [how to choose a biodata photo](/blog/how-to-choose-a-biodata-photo).

### Step 4: Write your personal introduction & partner preferences
Craft two to three warm, sincere sentences summarizing your lifestyle, values, and career outlook. Express your partner preferences clearly without listing rigid demands. Refer to our detailed guides on [writing About Me](/guides/how-to-write-about-me-in-biodata) and [partner expectations](/guides/how-to-write-partner-expectations).

### Step 5: Select a template & color palette
Click **Change Template** to preview your document rendered in 12 distinct template styles—ranging from traditional gold motifs to sleek modern indigo cards. You can change templates and color themes freely without losing any typed data.

### Step 6: Inspect the live A4 preview
Review every line carefully, paying close attention to phone numbers, dates, and spelling. On mobile devices, tap the **Preview** toggle to view the live A4 sheet layout.

### Step 7: Export & download
Choose your preferred file format:
- **Download PDF**: Ideal for high-resolution printing on A4 paper and emailing.
- **Download PNG / JPEG**: Perfect for sending via WhatsApp, Telegram, or messaging apps.

Your file is generated instantly in your browser and saved directly to your device downloads folder.
