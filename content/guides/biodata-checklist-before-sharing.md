Two minutes with this list can prevent most embarrassing mistakes.

## The checklist

1. **Name** is spelled the way it appears on official documents
2. **Date of birth** is correct, and the age shown matches it
3. **Time and place of birth** are right, if your family uses horoscopes
4. **Height and marital status** are accurate
5. **Education** lists your highest completed qualification
6. **Job title and company** are current
7. **Parents' names and occupations** are correct
8. **Siblings** are listed with the right relationship and status
9. **Phone number** is correct and will be answered
10. **Photo** is recent, clear and cropped well
11. **Nothing sensitive** such as ID numbers or a full address is included
12. **Preview** looks right on a phone and on a full page

## Read it like a stranger

Take a slow read from top to bottom. Could a family who has never met you understand who you are and how to reach you?

## Ask someone else

A parent or sibling will catch mistakes you have stopped seeing. Ask them to check names, dates and numbers.

## Then share

When the checklist is done, download the PDF or image from the [editor](/create). If you change anything later, download a fresh copy so old versions do not circulate.
