A well-printed biodata makes a good impression at a meeting. The steps are simple.

## 1. Download the PDF

In the [editor](/create), choose Download PDF. It is an A4 page created at about 300 dpi, so it prints sharply. If a multi-page biodata is created, each page is a full A4 sheet.

## 2. Open it and choose Print

Use any PDF viewer or your phone's share menu to print.

## 3. Check these settings

- **Paper size:** A4
- **Scale:** 100 percent or Actual size (not Fit or Shrink to fit)
- **Margins:** None or Default. The design already includes its own spacing.
- **Orientation:** Portrait
- **Quality:** Best or High

## 4. Pick the paper

Plain white paper of 80 to 100 gsm works well. For a formal meeting, a slightly heavier or light cream paper adds a touch of quality, especially with traditional templates.

## 5. Print one page first

Check the test print for cut-off edges and colours. Coloured backgrounds use more ink, so if you print many copies, a minimal template saves ink.

## If the download does not work

Some browsers restrict file creation. Use **Print or save as PDF** from the Download menu. It uses your browser's own print dialog and keeps text selectable.

## For a phone

Save the PDF, then use the Print option in your phone's share menu or take the file to a print shop on a pen drive or by email.
