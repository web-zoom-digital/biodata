A template does not change what your biodata says, but it changes how it feels. A few questions make the choice easy.

## 1. What will your family expect?

Traditional families often like a framed layout with gold or maroon accents. Younger families may prefer a quiet, modern design. Neither is better. Choose the one your reader will feel at home with.

## 2. Is it easy to read?

Open the preview on your phone. Text should be readable without zooming. Very ornate designs can look beautiful and still be hard to skim.

## 3. How much content do you have?

If you have many details, choose a two-column layout that uses space well. If you have less, a minimal template with more white space looks composed rather than empty.

## 4. Where does the photo sit?

Some designs place the photo at the top right, others in a sidebar or a centred arch. If your photo is a formal portrait, an arched or framed style suits it. A casual portrait sits well in a rounded or square frame.

## 5. Try before you decide

Use Change template in the [editor](/create) to see your own details in each design. It takes seconds and does not touch your data. Also browse the [template gallery](/templates) to compare styles at a glance.
