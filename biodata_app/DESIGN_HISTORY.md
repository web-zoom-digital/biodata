## Newly Created Unique Templates

### Template 1: Aurora Sage — Contemporary Elegance (`aurora-sage`)
- **Status:** Implemented (Current Request)
- **Primary / Secondary Colors:** Deep Eucalyptus Sage (`#3D5A4C`), Sandalwood Gold (`#C8A97E`), Alabaster Warm Cream (`#F9F7F2`), Espresso Charcoal (`#1E2A24`).
- **Layout Type:** Asymmetric Editorial Split-Hero + 4-Point Quick Highlights Badge Strip + Contemporary Modular Bento Grid.
- **Typography Style:** Modern High-Contrast Editorial Serif (Playfair Display 700 / Georgia) paired with clean geometric Plus Jakarta Sans.
- **Portrait Placement:** Top-Left Arched Pill/Capsule Cameo with double champagne ring border and subtle botanical leaf spray.
- **Decorative Theme:** Contemporary Scandinavian-Indian Fusion, minimalist champagne wireframes, micro-pills, and clean modular cards.
- **Main Distinguishing Features:**
  - Floating 4-item horizontal highlight badges strip (`[ 📍 Location ] [ 🎓 Education ] [ 💼 Profession ] [ 📏 Height ]`).
  - Distinctive pill-shaped (capsule arch) portrait frame.
  - Asymmetric modular card layout with two-tone micro-pills for astrology and cultural roots.
  - Large stylized serif quote block with architectural quote marks.
  - Left timeline connector for education and career milestones with date badges.
